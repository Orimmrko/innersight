Function by function, one per package

extraction/ Stage 1
Anything that touches a raw CSV. The five per-log builders, the fold, the join, and why distinct counts cannot be summed across chunks.
labeling/ Stage 2
The answer key and the calendar. Filling missing days with zeros, and marking which days fall inside an attack window.
baseline/ Stage 3
The thesis. Scoring each day against that person's own prior days, the standard-deviation floor, and the 2σ gate that decided the project was worth continuing.
splitting/ Stage 4
Who goes where. Assigning users rather than rows, stratified by attack type, across three seeds.
modeling/ Stages 5 + 6
Both models and everything they share — tensors, architectures, the training loop that knows nothing about which model it trains, the metrics, the orchestration.
evaluation/ Stage 7
Reads checkpoints, never trains. Scoring, detection latency, false-positive inspection, the report — and guards.py, eight functions each named for what it refuses to allow.
If you have ten minutes and nothing else. Read Where everything lives, then open evaluation/guards.py in the editor and read the eight function names. Those two things together answer most navigation and most integrity questions faster than any amount of memorising.
